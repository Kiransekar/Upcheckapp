Two accounts, one app
Home spans every farm you belong to — each alert, task and total carries the farm it came from, so nothing assumes a single farm. Farms and Ponds are the two levels below it, each opening with a short at-a-glance summary. Team holds the whole team's tasks; your own sit on Home. The owner set comes first, then the same screens as a worker sees them: fewer decisions, no Money tab, only his ponds. Today's app is recreated at the bottom for comparison.

Tapping a pond opens p1. The fourteen log types stay, but the six daily ones sit on the surface and the rest collapse to a line. Calculators (p2, p3) prefill from the pond you picked instead of asking you to type MBW and survival again. Simulations (p4, p5) keep the real scenario types, and Settings (p6) gets the tab it now has in the nav.
